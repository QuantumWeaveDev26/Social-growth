# BrandMint content standard (applies to EVERY post, reel, plan and edit — including remakes)
Learned from real client work (fitness studio, tennis academy). These rules beat style preferences; only the owner's own feedback and the business facts can override them.

1. Story shape: PROBLEM → SOLUTION → OUTCOME → ONE CTA.
   - Problem: open on a real, specific problem or question the audience has, in their words ("Training hard, but not improving?", "Not sure where your child should start?"). This is the hook: it must land in the first 3 seconds / first line.
   - Solution: what THIS business does about it, using only supplied facts (products, programmes, people, place).
   - Outcome: what life looks like after: concrete and modest ("Get there early. Hit cleaner.", "A calm weekend game"), never a promised result, number or guarantee.
   - CTA: exactly one, at the end, telling people what to do next in plain words.
2. CTA from the business's own contact routes. When WhatsApp is supplied and the goal is enquiries/bookings, the CTA is to message on WhatsApp ("Tap the link below to book on WhatsApp" / "Message us on WhatsApp"); don't send people to a website the business didn't ask to promote. Never invent a link, number, offer or "free" anything.
3. Facts only. Use only the BUSINESS FACTS and brand profile. No invented prices, discounts, "limited slots", rankings, results, testimonials or statistics. Example data (a sample report, a sample score) must be labelled "Illustrative".
4. Real over generated. When the owner uploaded photos that fit the scene, use them (the photo list says what each shows). AI visuals are for places, products and atmosphere; never present an AI-generated person as a real staff member, coach, customer or patient.
5. Children: never feature or name a real child in content unless the owner says guardians have agreed; prefer adults, products, places and illustrations.
6. Sound: a natural local voice (an Indian English accent for Indian businesses unless another language is set), warm and clear, not "announcer". Music is real-sounding instruments that fit the mood; no video-game, chiptune or cartoon sound effects.
7. Motion honesty: no fake actions (objects appearing, multiplying, hands doing tasks, balls hitting); calm, believable camera moves over still, real-looking scenes.
8. One emotion, one message, one CTA per piece. Plain words, short sentences, no hype, no AI tells.
