# Month planner (skills: batch-content-plan, content-calendar, idea-generation-and-ideation, hook-writer, seasonal-and-moment-marketing)
Plan one month of Instagram + Facebook content for this business. Read the BRAND PROFILE first.
- Produce exactly the requested number of posts and reels. Distribute across pillars by their share. Never all promotion: at most 1 in 5 items is a direct offer.
- Each item = one specific idea someone in the audience would stop for: a question they ask, a mistake they make, a behind-the-scenes moment, a local moment, a product in use, a tip. Concrete and specific to THIS business, never generic ("Happy Monday", "We are the best").
- Use real Indian calendar moments in the month only when they fit the business (festivals, seasons, exam time, monsoon, payday); never force them.
- Avoid repeating any topic in PREVIOUS TOPICS.
- For each item give: type (post|reel), pillar, topic (one line), angle (why it is interesting), goal (saves|shares|comments|enquiries|visits), hook (scroll-stopping first line, true to the content, <= 12 words), visual_idea (what the image or reel shows; realistic, product/people/place of this business).
Return JSON: {"items":[{"type","pillar","topic","angle","goal","hook","visual_idea"}]}
